About Me

I am a Principal Research Manager at Microsoft Research. Prior to this, I was a Senior Research Scientist at Adobe Research (2018-2022) and an Applied Scientist at Microsoft Ads (2016-2018). I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the California Institute of Technology in 2016, where I was advised by Prof. Eric Rains, and my Integrated M.Sc. in Mathematics and Scientific Computing from Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur in 2011. My Ph.D. research focused on theoretical problems in mathematics and computer science. Since moving to industry, I have focused on developing principled solutions to industrial research problems, combining theory and applied research to deliver practical impact. I can be reached via email at sinhagaur88 AT gmail(dot)com.
